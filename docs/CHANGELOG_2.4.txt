Playable Creatures 2.4

All races
- Every race except the robots and the Zetans now starts with the Terrifying Presence perk (given when the race menu closes).
- Forward power attacks with melee weapons now play for every race. Melee weapons use their own special power attacks, which none of the races had.
- Unarmed special-move perks (Legion Assault, Ranger Takedown, Khan Trick, Scribe Counter) now play each race's matching power attack.
- Fixed attacks freezing after a melee hit bounced off a block or another power attack. Every race now has the missing Recoil animations.

Ghost People
- Gas spray (back power attack): sprays gas from the mask. It knocks the one target it hits to the ground for a few seconds. It works unarmed and with melee weapons, including the unarmed perk moves.
- Forward power attack is the Dead Money charging lunge again.
- New back dodge: with a weapon drawn, back + jump. The Ghost vanishes into a cloud of its gas, slips back and re-forms.
- New unarmed attacks: the feral ghoul's punches, adapted to the Ghost's body.
- Ghost Gas heavy weapon:
  - a thicker, see-through gas stream with a softer impact puff;
  - no longer flagged as a backpack weapon;
  - aimed casts no longer put the palm against the mask.
- Grenades and throwing spears are sheathed on the back, as the Dead Money ghosts carry them, not on the hip.
- The two-handed gas spray holds the spear in one hand, as the ghosts do.

Super Mutants, Nightkin and Capital Super Mutants (TTW)
- One-handed melee weapons now use their own unarmed attacks, with the weapon unseen. Reach, damage and effects still apply.
- New taunt: with a weapon drawn, back + jump plays an intimidation display with a combat bark.
  - Taunt within 10 seconds of a kill and they gloat instead.
  - Nightkin speak with their own voice.

Core (PlayableCreatures.dll)
- New settings: GivePerks, TauntIdles, TauntSound, TauntKillSound.
- Every race also has UnarmedEffectWeapons, UnarmedEffectReachMult and UnarmedEffectCone, off except for the Ghost People.
- Ghost melee effects (gas spray reach and cone) are part of Core. There is no separate DLL.
